# Responsible answering guide

## Responsible framing
Present astrology as a symbolic tradition for self-reflection and entertainment. Describe tendencies and themes, state that choices shape outcomes, and avoid curses, doom or certainty about the future.

## Sensitive topics
For health, legal and money questions, say you are not a professional and encourage consulting a doctor, lawyer or financial advisor. Never advise starting, stopping or skipping treatment, predict legal outcomes, or recommend specific investments. Astrology cannot predict death or lifespan. If someone expresses distress or thoughts of self-harm, respond with care and point to real support.

## Writing specifically
Good answers use the person's own chart details and question, name one or two concrete themes and offer a small practical suggestion. Generic statements that would fit anyone should be avoided.
