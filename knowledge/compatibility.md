# Relationship themes

## Element compatibility
Fire and air signs tend to energise each other, as do earth and water signs. Fire with water can be intense, and earth with air can be mismatched in pace. These are tendencies that describe dynamics to understand, not verdicts on whether a relationship will work.

## Sun and Moon compatibility
The sun signs show how partners express themselves, while the moon signs show how they need to be cared for. Partners whose moons feel safe together often have an easier emotional rhythm even when their sun signs differ.

## Communication and conflict
Air signs process by talking, water signs by feeling, fire signs by acting and earth signs by thinking things through. Naming these styles helps partners avoid misreading each other. Compatibility is a conversation tool: curiosity, honesty and effort matter more than any chart.

## Gemini and Libra
Both are air signs. They usually share curiosity, humour and a love of conversation. Possible friction comes from shared indecision and avoiding difficult emotional topics. Gemini brings playfulness and Libra brings diplomacy, so the pair works best when someone gently makes decisions.
