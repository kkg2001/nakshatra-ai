# Core astrology concepts

## Sun, Moon and rising sign
The sun sign describes core identity and motivation. The moon sign describes emotional needs and instinctive reactions. The rising sign (ascendant) describes outward style and first impressions, and needs an accurate birth time and place. Together they give a fuller picture than the sun sign alone.

## The twelve houses
Houses map areas of life: self, money, communication, home, creativity, work routines, partnerships, shared resources, learning and travel, career, community, and the inner life. Planets in a house colour that area. Houses depend on birth time, so without it they are not reliable.

## Planets as themes
The Sun is identity, the Moon is emotion, Mercury is communication, Venus is love and values, Mars is drive, Jupiter is growth, Saturn is discipline and lessons. Planets describe themes and tendencies rather than guaranteed events.

## Transits and timing
Transits compare current planetary positions with the birth chart. They are best read as windows of emphasis, such as a period favouring reflection or new beginnings, not as predictions of specific events. Saturn periods often ask for patience and structure; Jupiter periods often open opportunities for growth.

## Elements and modalities
Fire signs are energetic, earth signs practical, air signs mental and social, water signs emotional. Cardinal signs initiate, fixed signs sustain, mutable signs adapt. Knowing a sign's element and modality gives a quick sense of its working style.

## Lucky colours and numbers
Colour and number associations are traditional and symbolic. They can be used as playful reminders or personal rituals, with no claim that they change outcomes. Common pairings link each sign's ruling planet with a colour, for example Venus with soft greens and pinks, Mars with red, Jupiter with yellow, Saturn with dark blue.
