# Western sun signs (original summaries for retrieval)

## Aries
Fire sign, cardinal modality, ruled by Mars. Direct, energetic and competitive, Aries learns by doing and likes to start things. Strengths are courage and momentum. Growth edge: impatience and finishing what is started. In love, honest and passionate. Careers with autonomy suit it: leadership, entrepreneurship, sports.

## Taurus
Earth sign, fixed modality, ruled by Venus. Steady, sensual and loyal, Taurus values comfort, security and builds slowly. Strengths are reliability and patience. Growth edge: stubbornness and resisting change. In love, devoted and needs consistency. Careers in finance, craft, food, design or any long-horizon work suit it.

## Gemini
Air sign, mutable modality, ruled by Mercury. Curious, talkative and adaptable, Gemini thrives on variety and ideas. Strengths are communication and quick learning. Growth edge: scattered focus and restlessness. In love, needs mental stimulation and humour. Careers in writing, teaching, media, sales or technology suit it.

## Cancer
Water sign, cardinal modality, ruled by the Moon. Nurturing, intuitive and protective, Cancer is centred on home, family and emotional safety. Strengths are empathy and care. Growth edge: moodiness and guardedness. In love, seeks security and deep bonds. Careers in caregiving, hospitality, real estate or HR suit it.

## Leo
Fire sign, fixed modality, ruled by the Sun. Warm, generous and expressive, Leo wants to create, lead and be appreciated. Strengths are confidence and loyalty. Growth edge: pride and needing validation. In love, romantic and wholehearted. Careers in performance, leadership, creative direction or teaching suit it.

## Virgo
Earth sign, mutable modality, ruled by Mercury. Analytical, practical and helpful, Virgo notices details and likes improving systems. Strengths are diligence and discernment. Growth edge: perfectionism, self-criticism and overwork. In love, shows care through acts of service. Careers in analysis, operations, editing, quality assurance or support roles suit it.

## Libra
Air sign, cardinal modality, ruled by Venus. Diplomatic, fair-minded and aesthetic, Libra values harmony and partnership. Strengths are tact and cooperation. Growth edge: indecision and people-pleasing. In love, seeks balance and shared experiences. Careers in mediation, design, HR, law-adjacent or diplomatic work suit it.

## Scorpio
Water sign, fixed modality, traditionally ruled by Mars. Intense, private and perceptive, Scorpio seeks depth and truth. Strengths are determination and emotional insight. Growth edge: control, suspicion and holding grudges. In love, loyal and wants intimacy. Careers in research, investigation, psychology, strategy or finance suit it.

## Sagittarius
Fire sign, mutable modality, ruled by Jupiter. Optimistic, adventurous and philosophical, Sagittarius loves freedom, learning and travel. Strengths are enthusiasm and vision. Growth edge: bluntness and overcommitting. In love, needs space and shared adventure. Careers in education, travel, publishing or consulting suit it.

## Capricorn
Earth sign, cardinal modality, ruled by Saturn. Disciplined, ambitious and responsible, Capricorn is a patient builder who plays the long game. Strengths are persistence and practicality. Growth edge: workaholism and emotional reserve. In love, slow to open but deeply committed. Careers in management, engineering, finance or long projects suit it. Later-life career changes often benefit from its steady planning.

## Aquarius
Air sign, fixed modality, associated with Saturn and Uranus. Independent, inventive and humanitarian, Aquarius values originality and community ideas. Strengths are vision and open-mindedness. Growth edge: emotional detachment and fixed opinions. In love, friendship comes first. Feeling like an outsider in groups is a common theme; it often eases when it finds like-minded communities. Careers in technology, research or social causes suit it.

## Pisces
Water sign, mutable modality, associated with Jupiter and Neptune. Empathetic, imaginative and intuitive, Pisces absorbs the moods around it. Strengths are compassion and creativity. Growth edge: boundaries and escapism. In love, romantic and giving. Careers in the arts, music, counselling support or healing-adjacent work suit it.
