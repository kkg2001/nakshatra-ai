# Vedic (Jyotish) basics

## Rashi and lagna
In Jyotish the zodiac sign of a planet is its rashi, and the rising sign is the lagna. The Moon rashi is often treated as the most important placement for personality and emotional life, alongside the lagna for overall life direction.

## Nakshatras
The sky is divided into 27 lunar mansions called nakshatras, each spanning 13 degrees 20 minutes and split into four padas. The nakshatra of the Moon at birth is used to describe temperament, instincts and compatibility. For example Rohini is linked with nurturing, growth and attraction to beauty and stability.

## Dashas
Dashas are planetary periods that provide a timing framework. The common Vimshottari system spans 120 years and assigns each planet a stretch of life, based on the Moon's nakshatra. Each period highlights that planet's themes, such as discipline in Saturn periods or learning in Jupiter periods. They suggest emphasis, not fixed events.

## Sidereal and tropical zodiac
Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac tied to the seasons, while Jyotish uses the sidereal zodiac tied to the stars, usually with the Lahiri ayanamsa. The two differ by about 24 degrees, so a person's Vedic sun sign is often the sign before their Western one. Both are symbolic systems with different conventions.

## Simple habits instead of costly remedies
Traditional remedies include meditation, mantra repetition, fasting, charity and service. They are best presented as supportive habits for focus and reflection. Expensive gemstones or elaborate rituals are not necessary, and no remedy replaces practical action or professional advice.
